Problems trainees face — solved
Lost to follow-up when phone changes
Multi-token identity (Aadhaar token + name-DOB fuzzy match + last known employer) allows re-linkage even after SIM change. Field counsellors re-verify in person.
No portable skills credential
Trainee outcome card, a shareable QR-linked record showing certifications, employment history and verified wage history, works like a skills passport.
Placed in wrong-fit jobs
Demand-signal integration from employers flags sector/role mismatches before placement. Re-skilling nudges trigger automatically if job is below training level.
No grievance channel
In-app and IVR grievance logging during follow-up calls, escalation path to district officer, tracked to resolution with SLA timers.
Wage exploitation after placement
Wage benchmark alerts compare reported earnings against sector median at 6 and 12 months. Anomalies trigger counsellor outreach.
Self-employed and gig workers invisible
Dedicated self-employment module captures business registration, GSTIN, UdyamCard, platform earnings (Swiggy, Urban Company API) and micro-enterprise credit access.
Follow-up protocol by channel
IVR / phone — tier 1
Automated call at 3, 6, 12, 24 months. 8 questions max. Dual-language. Response logged and classified automatically. 72-hour retry if unanswered.
WhatsApp — tier 2
Button-response survey if IVR unanswered after 2 attempts. Handles richer self-employment data, photo uploads of business, salary slips.
Counsellor app — tier 3
Offline-capable Android app for field staff. GPS-verified home visit. Face-match re-verification. Handles trainees unreachable by digital means.
Administrative pull — tier 4
For non-responders after 3 attempts: EPF/ESIC contribution lookup, UAN status, state employment exchange data — passive signal without burdening the trainee.
