What this system does
Consent-based longitudinal identity
Persistent trainee ID across programmes, phone changes, and geography using Aadhaar token plus fuzzy biographic match, never raw biometrics.
Multi-channel automated follow-up
IVR, WhatsApp, SMS and field counsellor app adapts channel by response rate, covering employed, self-employed, apprentice and dropout outcomes.
Administrative signal validation
EPF/ESIC contribution cross-checks, UAN lookup and DigiLocker certificate verification replace self-reported employer data with ground truth.
Wage and retention progression
Tracks earnings and job continuity at 3, 6, 12, 24 and 36 months, not just placement at day zero.
Skill-gap and attrition diagnosis
Classifies non-placement and dropout by root cause: demand-side, supply-side, geographic, or personal, enabling targeted remediation.
Evidence-based policy outputs
Cohort, course, provider, district and demographic analytics with open-data portal, ministerial dashboard and individual trainee cards.
Who benefits
Trainee / employee
Portable outcome record, grievance channel, re-skilling nudges, wage benchmarks, and career counselling triggers when employment stalls.
Employer
Verified candidate quality signal, apprenticeship subsidy claims simplified, retention analytics, and demand-signal integration for future cohort planning.
Training provider
Outcome-linked performance scores, benchmark against peers, course improvement recommendations, and protected appeal process for anomalies.
Ministry / district
Real-time placement dashboards, fund-flow linked to validated outcomes, skill-gap maps, and evidence base for policy revision.
